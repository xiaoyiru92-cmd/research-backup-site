What this utility does

PSFC Research Backup transfers and manages user-selected backups and associated metadata in the user’s own Google Drive. It works only with files the app created or files the user explicitly opened with or shared with the app.

  • Back up selected research files chosen by the user.
  • Manage those app-associated backups and their metadata at the user’s direction.
  • Store backup content in the user’s own Google Drive account, hosted by Google.

Limited Google Drive scope

The app requests the narrow drive.file scope:

Only files created by the app, or explicitly opened with or shared with the app. It does not request access to the user’s whole Drive.

What this website is

Informational onlyThis site describes the utility and its privacy practices. It does not run backups.
No sign-in hereThis site has no OAuth callback, no sign-in, and no credential collection. It has no access to any backup host.

How authorization works

Google authorization and backup transfers are handled by the operator’s separate authorization and backup tools — not by these static web pages. OAuth credentials are processed by those operator tools and are never published on this site.

Data handling at a glance

  • Backup files and metadata live in the user’s Google Drive under the user’s account.
  • Backups remain until the user deletes them.
  • No advertising and no sale of personal information.
  • GitHub Pages hosting may process visitor IP addresses and request logs under GitHub’s privacy policy.
  • No absolute security guarantees are made.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Managing or removing data

  • Revoke the app’s Drive access at myaccount.google.com/connections. Revoking access does not delete existing backups.
  • Delete backup files and empty the Drive trash yourself in Google Drive.
  • For removal of app-managed credentials or data held by the operator, contact thomassendrue755@gmail.com.
