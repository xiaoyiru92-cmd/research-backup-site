PSFC Research Backup — Privacy Policy

PSFC Research Backup is a personally operated research backup utility. It is explicitly not an official MIT or PSFC service.

1. About this policy and this site

This policy describes how the PSFC Research Backup utility handles information when it transfers and manages user-selected backups in Google Drive.

This website is informational only. It provides descriptions of the utility and this policy. It has no OAuth callback, collects no credentials, and has no access to any backup host.

2. What the app does

The app uses authorized Google Drive access to transfer and manage user-selected backups and associated metadata in the user’s own Google Drive. Google hosts Drive data under the user’s account, subject to Google’s terms and privacy policy.

3. Google Drive scope

The app uses the narrow drive.file scope. That means access is limited to:

  • files the app created, or
  • files the user explicitly opened with or shared with the app.
The app does not request access to the user’s whole Drive.

4. Credentials and authorization

OAuth credentials are processed by the operator’s authorization and backup tools and are never published on this site. Never send passwords, tokens, or credential files by email or publish them in issues or chat.

This policy does not claim any particular at-rest encryption implementation for operator-held material, and does not claim automated retention or deletion automation beyond what is stated in Section 6.

5. Information use, advertising, and sale

  • User-selected backups and associated metadata are used only to provide, maintain, and troubleshoot the requested backup function.
  • There is no advertising and no sale of personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Backups stored in the user’s Google Drive remain until the user deletes them.

  1. To stop future access, revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/connections. Revoking access does not delete existing backups.
  2. To delete backup content, delete the relevant Drive files and empty the Drive trash yourself.
  3. To request removal of app-managed credentials or other data held by the operator, contact thomassendrue755@gmail.com.

7. Website hosting and visitor information

This static site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages hosting may process visitor IP addresses and request logs needed to serve the site, under GitHub’s privacy policy. This site itself uses no scripts, no external assets, no forms, and no trackers.

8. Security

Reasonable care is used in operating the utility, but no absolute security guarantees are made. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or removal requests, contact the operator at thomassendrue755@gmail.com.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
